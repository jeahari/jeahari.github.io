Gas & Service Log

Effective November 18, 2026 · Published by JeaHari LLC

The short version

You can use Gas & Service Log without an account; then everything stays on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google to share with family, your records are stored in our cloud database so your family members can see them. We never sell your data, show ads or use analytics, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. What the app stores

  • Vehicles: the name and type you give each car, an optional dealer or shop name, and an optional photo you pick
  • Fuel records: date and time, odometer reading, gas station, price per gallon and total paid
  • Maintenance records: date, odometer reading, service provider, type of service and price paid
  • If you sign in: your Google account's name and email address, and which family group you belong to

2. Where it is stored

Without signing in: everything is kept in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent to us.

After signing in: your records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Google Firebase), which JeaHari LLC uses as its database provider. Data is encrypted in transit. Records you had on the phone before signing in are moved into your account.

3. Who can see it

Only you and the people in your family group. Someone joins your family only with a 6-character invite code that you create; codes work once and expire after 24 hours. Members can see and edit the family's cars and records, and see each member's name and email. We do not share your data with anyone else, except if required by law.

4. Backups and exports you create

You can save a backup file or a CSV export to a place you choose, such as your phone or Google Drive. Those files are handled by that service under its own terms. Android may also include the app's on-phone data in your device's Google backup if you have that turned on.

5. Permissions

The app uses the internet only for sign-in and syncing. It does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone. When you add a vehicle photo or restore a backup, Android's own file picker opens and the app only reads the item you pick.

6. Deleting your data

  • Delete any record or car inside the app at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app: 👤 Family → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your sign-in data. If you were the only member of your family group, all of its cars and records are deleted too; otherwise the records stay with the remaining family members.
  • Leaving a family keeps its records with the family.
  • You can also ask us to delete your account by email — see how to delete your account.
  • Without an account, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage erases its data from your phone.

7. Children

The app is intended for adult drivers and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
