Privacy Policy

Gas & Service Log

Effective November 18, 2026 · Published by JeaHari LLC

The short version

Gas & Service Log keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

1. What the app stores

When you use the app, it saves the following on your device only:

  • Fuel records: date and time, odometer reading, gas station, price per gallon and total paid
  • Maintenance records: date, odometer reading, service provider, type of service and price paid
  • Optional photos of your vehicles that you choose from your device

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to JeaHari or to anyone else.

2. Backups and exports you create

The app lets you save a backup file or export a CSV file. You choose where that file goes (for example your phone's storage or your Google Drive). Those files are handled by the place you save them, under that service's own privacy terms. JeaHari never receives them.

Android may also include the app's data in your device's own Google backup if you have device backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by JeaHari.

3. Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. When you add a vehicle photo or restore a backup, Android's own file picker opens and the app only reads the item you pick.

4. Children

The app is intended for adult drivers and is not directed at children.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete any record inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently erases all of its data from your phone. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore data for you, so keep a backup file if you want one.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
