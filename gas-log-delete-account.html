Account deletion

Delete your Gas & Service Log account

Gas & Service Log · Published by JeaHari LLC

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  • Open Gas & Service Log and tap 👤 Family on the My Cars screen.
  • Tap Delete account, tap it again to confirm, then confirm with your Google account.

Your account is deleted right away.

Or ask us by email

Email Jeaharichang@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Gas & Service Log account”. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, name and email address in our database.
  • If you are the only member of your family group: all of its cars, photos, fuel and service records.
  • If other family members remain: you are removed from the family, and the shared records stay with them.

Nothing is kept after deletion, other than what the law requires. Backup or CSV files you saved yourself are not affected.
